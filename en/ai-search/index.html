Services

Be seen when customers ask an AI

More and more people ask an AI assistant instead of searching. It answers with what it can read.

AI services like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity fetch answers from the web. They favour pages that are easy to read, have clear facts and answer questions directly. Much of the work resembles good SEO, but some parts are new.

What is done

  • robots.txt that lets AI search bots in
  • Short, fact-based answers to common questions
  • Structured data about the business and services
  • The same facts wherever you are mentioned

Good to know

  • Nobody can control exactly what an AI answers
  • What helps AI search usually helps ordinary search too
  • No invented claims; only facts that can be checked

Frequently asked questions

Which AI bots are let in?

For example OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User, PerplexityBot plus Googlebot and Bingbot.

Can we block AI bots that train models?

Yes. Search bots and training bots can be handled separately in robots.txt.
