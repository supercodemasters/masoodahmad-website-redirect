Services

Be found when people search locally

People searching for your service in Örebro should find you, not just the big chains.

Local searches like “electrician Örebro” or “accounting firm near me” are decided by different things than ordinary searches. It is about clear city pages, a well-kept business profile and the same name and contact details everywhere.

What is included

  • Pages per service and town, with their own content
  • A review of your Google business profile
  • The same name and details on the website and directories
  • Structured data for local businesses

Good to know

  • Reviews affect local visibility; we can make it easier for happy customers to leave them
  • Fake reviews are never used
  • You do not have to show your address if you visit customers

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a physical address to be found locally?

No. Businesses that visit customers can show a service area instead of an address.

Does it work outside Örebro?

Yes. The same method works for every town you work in.
