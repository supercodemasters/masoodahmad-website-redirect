Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee a top spot?

No. Nobody honestly can. I do what can be influenced and measure the result.

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes often show within a few weeks; content and local visibility take longer.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size and state of the website. After the review you get a proposal with a price.

Is AI search different from SEO?

Partly. The base is the same, but AI services favour short factual answers and clear structured data.
