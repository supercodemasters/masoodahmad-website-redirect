Faster pages, better rankings

Speed is one of the few things on Google that can be measured exactly.

Google measures how fast pages load and how stable they are while loading. Slow pages lose visitors and rankings. I measure, find the biggest brakes and fix them.

Common brakes

  • Images that are too large and in old formats
  • Heavy themes and plugins
  • Third-party scripts that load first
  • Fonts loaded from external servers

How it is measured

  • Measurement on mobile and desktop before and after
  • The same measures Google uses (Core Web Vitals)
  • A short report of what was changed

Frequently asked questions
